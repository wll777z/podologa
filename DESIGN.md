# Design — Sueli

## Direction
Clinical precision with human warmth, as defined in the supplied 2.AG reference. Public landing page persuades; the local panel operates. Preserve the split dark editorial/light photograph hero, adapted to foot care rather than supplement packaging.

## Tokens
- Deep Teal #244d54; Mint Pulse #2ecea0; Soft Teal #6dddbd.
- White #ffffff; Bone #f0f1f2; Mist #e5e7eb; Ink #151515; Graphite #4d4d4d.
- Inter Tight, weights 400/500/600; 62px main display, 45px section display, 16px body.
- Pill actions, 12–20px service corners, 5px inputs. Hairline borders, no shadows.
- Use deep teal text on mint for legible actions; this accessibility adjustment preserves the supplied palette.

## Composition
Mint announcement, white sticky navigation, balanced split hero, compact care strip, dense editorial service grid, full-width care explorer, treatment process, inline appointment form, useful FAQ and contact/settings-aware footer. The professional name carries identity, not an invented clinic brand.

## Motion thesis
The principal visual is a mint wireframe foot with a scanning band and selectable care points; selecting a point reveals its contextual description. All factual content stays visible without animation. Supporting motion explains tab selection, form progress and request status. No repeated fade-up choreography. Pause loops offscreen and when hidden. Honor reduced motion.

## Honest local behavior
Local storage, deliberate consent, visible save failure, exported requests via download, explicit confirmation that no message has been sent. Optional WhatsApp handoff is a user-initiated link to a configured real number. No hidden telemetry or remote submission.

## Atualização de identidade e escopo
Materiais e logo em creme/dourado foram fornecidos pelo usuário e recriados conforme sua autorização, preservando a estrutura teal/menta do site. A apresentação fica em Sobre, técnicas em Terapias, contato junto aos botões WhatsApp e logo em cabeçalho/rodapé. Ampliação nativa em diálogo funciona também no HTML independente. O formulário e o painel foram retirados: agendamento exclusivamente pelo WhatsApp oficial fornecido nos materiais.
